# Growth Audit — Sprouty (v2)

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/sprouty-flow-rebuild-v2/

Your Meta ads tell a tired parent "turns out that's just this week". The first paywall then shows a sample baby at week 42. This audit has five ideas and a clickable prototype of your onboarding and paywall, rebuilt in Sprouty's own design, with one first launch per ad: your leap ad, your wake-window ad and your Apple Ads brand search. Only the order of the screens changes. The colours, controls, bunny, wheels, trial timeline and plan picker are Sprouty's own.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Open the trial on their baby, not a sample | CR +15–20% |
| 02 | Ask about the baby before the account | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 03 | Ask for notifications when you promise the reminder | CR +10–15% |
| 04 | Show the calendar you just built | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Put your 3,000,000+ parents next to the price | CR +10–15% |

The prototype shows three phones side by side, one per ad, with a light/dark switch (dark as the app was recorded, light from the app screens in your ads), EN / DE / FR, and a 30-second walkthrough. Closing the paywall goes straight into the app.

**Grounding.** Prices, screens and copy come from the Sprouty iOS app as recorded (EU storefront: 5,99€ a month, 39,99€ a year with 7 days free). Rating, user count and the quoted review come from the US App Store listing (20 November 2026). Ads and Apple Ads terms come from AppMagic ad intelligence. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps, not measured lift for Sprouty.

The first audit is still live at https://bterzic123.github.io/sprouty-flow-rebuild/.
